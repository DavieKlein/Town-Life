city_animals.glb contains three models extracted from the pack (GroundedPigeon, Pigeon,
GraySquirrel), exported from the pack's FBX without changes to the meshes. In the game they are
posed (tilted, wings bent), rendered to small sprites and recoloured to the GBA palette.
