# Town Life - Story Overview (one page)

Status: writing proposal for the owner. Details: `docs/story_chapter1.md` (incl. section 3B), `docs/story_chapter2.md`,
`docs/story_chapters3-5.md`. Own characters only; light and funny, with serious moments.

## The arc in one paragraph
{NAME} is fired on day one when billionaire **Victor Vault** buys Corner Market and turns it into the cold **VaultMart**. With $20 and a park
bench, {NAME} slowly builds a life: a dish job, odd jobs, Old Block's neighbours, a steady job on the Docks, then a business, a team, a
community. Each of the five types (Grafters, Makers, Techies, Ballers, Posh) teaches a craft and becomes an ally. Vault's "Smart City"
(robots, self-checkouts, a city brain called AURUM, the demolition of Old Block) grows meaner every chapter, until the allies, an
old co-op charter and Vault's own recorded words undo it. Rich, in the end, means who you share the table with.

## Rival (Dex) arc
Ch.1 five small beats (sorry look, tired smile, the certificate he does not report, silence at the bridge, "No idea.") -> Ch.2 Regional
Manager, brings AutoPort, quietly sabotages the race ("sensor dust"), eats Rosa's bread -> Ch.3 Smile Index, a real smile, learns he is a
"placeholder" -> Ch.4 replaced by a robot manager and fired (turning point) -> Ch.5 three paths by `dex_decency` and friendship: redeemed
(joins the chain, works at Quill & Solder), grey (small shop), cold (leaves; a postcard).

## Villain (Vault) arc
Buys the shop -> closes the bridge -> void demolition notice -> AutoPort -> DataVault -> VaultPass/HealthPods -> machine-signed notice #2
-> buys the football club and a council vote -> the Gala and the AURUM Switch. Downfall: the 1931 Co-op Charter makes the sale void; the
Co-op tokens (Ch.1 collectibles) are the proof; Vault's own recording ("People are furniture") plays at the Gala; Valet, who cannot lie,
testifies; the hearing voids the notice; the human chain stops the digger; at the end his gold card fails at a self-checkout and Rosa
gives him a shift stocking shelves at the warm Corner Market. Tower becomes a public library.

## Themes
1. **Poor to rich = who you become:** every step up asks for help given or taken (the lent room, the co-op tokens).
2. **Warm human city vs. cold automation:** palettes turn warm area by area; machines are not evil, Vault's use of them is (Nadia, Valet).
3. **Community as property:** the Co-op Charter, the penthouse that nobody locks.
4. **Rivals and truce:** Grafters/Posh and Ballers/Techies mentors share a table (M49, M59) and the half-speed rule ends.

## Systems in the story
Needs and sickness (Okafor, the faint), Social (parties, Repair Café), friendship + City Coins (the co-op token "pay later"), gifts and
gossip (each chapter adds rules), phone texts (one nudge per mission), collectibles (coins = Co-op lore, postcards = Mia's family, flyers =
resistance), events (Saturday market, march, Block Party, Repair Café, Lantern Night, Silent Protest, Cup Match, Gala, Hearing).
